Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how our application collects, uses, and protects user data.

Data We Collect

Our application does not collect personal data from users. We do not request names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any other information that can identify users.

Anonymous Usage Data and Analytics

To improve the application and enhance user experience, we utilize Firebase Analytics, a service provided by Google, to collect anonymous usage data. This data collection is designed to help us understand how users interact with our application and identify areas for improvement.

The anonymous usage data we collect may include, but is not limited to:

  • Application usage frequency and session duration
  • Feature usage patterns and interactions with content
  • Device information (such as device type, operating system version, and screen resolution) - all anonymized
  • General geographic location data (country/region level only, not precise location)
  • App performance metrics and crash reports

All data collected through Firebase Analytics is anonymous and cannot be used to identify individual users. This information is aggregated and analyzed to help us improve app functionality, fix technical issues, and optimize the user experience. We do not combine this anonymous usage data with any personal information, and it cannot be traced back to you as an individual.

Advertising Attribution and Measurement

Our iOS application integrates the Meta (Facebook) Software Development Kit ("Meta SDK") solely for the purpose of measuring the effectiveness of our advertising campaigns. We use Apple's SKAdNetwork framework, a privacy-preserving attribution system built into iOS that provides only aggregated, anonymous campaign performance data.

Through SKAdNetwork, ad networks may receive confirmation that our app was installed following an ad impression, along with a campaign identifier and an optional conversion value. No personally identifiable information, device identifiers (such as IDFA), or individual user-level data is shared through this mechanism. We do not request App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission for this purpose, and we do not track individual users across apps or websites.

The Meta SDK may also collect certain technical information, including crash data for stability purposes and anonymized analytics data. This information is processed in aggregate form and cannot be used to identify individual users.

For more information about how Meta handles data, please visit Meta's Privacy Policy. For more information about SKAdNetwork, please refer to Apple's SKAdNetwork documentation.

Third-Party Services

Our application integrates with the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Analytics (provided by Google) — for anonymous usage analytics and app improvement. Firebase Analytics processes anonymous usage data in accordance with Google's privacy policies.
  • Meta (Facebook) SDK (provided by Meta Platforms, Inc.) — used exclusively for advertising attribution through Apple's SKAdNetwork. This provides only aggregated, anonymous campaign-level data and does not enable individual user tracking.

We may share aggregated, non-personally identifiable information with these advertising partners to measure the performance of our advertising campaigns. This data is processed through Apple's SKAdNetwork framework and Meta's Aggregated Event Measurement protocol, both designed to provide campaign-level insights without identifying individual users. The application contains no advertisements, and we do not share any information that could be used to personally identify you with these partners for tracking purposes.

Data Security

Although we do not collect personal data, we take all reasonable measures to ensure that all data within the application is secure and protected from unauthorized access. Firebase employs industry-standard security measures to protect the anonymous usage data it processes on our behalf.

Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to understand how your data is being used. Since we only collect anonymous usage data that cannot identify you personally, there is no personal information to access, modify, or delete. If you have concerns about anonymous analytics data collection, you may contact us using the information provided in the Contact section below.

Children's Privacy

This application is intended for children and does not collect personal data. We respect laws protecting children online, including the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). The anonymous usage data we collect through Firebase Analytics does not include any information that can identify children, and we do not knowingly collect, store, or share any personal data from children. Our advertising measurement is conducted exclusively through Apple's SKAdNetwork, which provides only aggregated, anonymous campaign data and does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information about individual users, including children. No behavioral profiles are created, and no targeted advertising is served based on individual user activity.

Changes to the Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable laws. All changes will be posted within the application or on our official website. We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your privacy.
